About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber and technology risk roles in regulated and high-consequence environments.

Through collaboration with leading UK universities and industry partners, IDR combines academic insight with real-world projects to develop highly capable digital risk professionals.

Our Model

Academy

Training programs and bootcamps designed for students and professionals entering digital and cyber risk careers.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing AI governance models, digital risk frameworks and future security technologies.

Advisory Services

Helping organisations implement frameworks like NIST, ISO 27001 and NIS2.
